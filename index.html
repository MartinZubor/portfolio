PROFIL

I studied traditional painting at an art high school, focusing on old master techniques and developing a strong foundation in drawing and visual storytelling. I later graduated from Matej Bel University
in Banská Bystrica. My work focuses on illustration, comics, and narrative-driven visuals. I enjoy building stories through composition, emotion, and atmosphere. I work as a freelancer using Adobe tools and Procreate, combining traditional drawing thinking with digital workflows.
